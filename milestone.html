2. Preliminary Results & Validation

~1e-14 Relative Momentum Error

Linear momentum is conserved to machine precision in a closed box through collisions; all three test scenes stay stable over 3 s of simulated time.

0.2–0.4 ms/substep (CPU Baseline)

Measured for 1.8k–3.9k particles (~30 fps at 80 substeps/frame, ~0.5× real time at dt = 2e-4 s), motivating our move to WebGPU compute.
